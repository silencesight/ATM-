# Didactic ESL ATM - v4.0.0 (ATM 4.0)

Welcome to the **Didactic ESL ATM Application (ATM 4.0)**! Built specifically for classroom educators to seamlessly manage a digital classroom token economy, loan mechanics, store privileges, and financial literacy inside a professional **Liquid Glass Bank Management** desktop workspace.

---

## 🚀 What's New in Version 4.0.0 (ATM 4.0)

### 💎 Prismatic & Crystalline Liquid Glass Bank Interface
- **Dual-Mode Liquid Glass Design System:**
  - **Dark Mode (`Prismatic Aero Glass`):** Deep obsidian space (`#0b0e17`), frosted translucent panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(190%)`), specular top borders, and Laser Cyan (`#00f0ff`), Aurora Emerald (`#65f2b5`), and Spectral Indigo (`#6366f1`) accents.
  - **Light Mode (`Aero Crystalline Glass`):** Projector-ready alabaster frost (`#f0f4fa`) with sapphire, emerald, and violet glass highlights for brightly lit classrooms.
- **Professional Bank Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans headlines, Inter UI body, and JetBrains Mono tabular financial ledgers.

### 🏦 Teacher Hub & Active Student Instruments
- **Refractive KPI Telemetry Ribbon:** Real-time Total Eurodollar Circulation, Active Student Instruments, Credit Velocity & FICO Average, and Active Loan Portfolio with interactive cohort filters.
- **Full-Width Student Identity & Dedicated 6-Button Utility Bar:**
  - Student names wrap cleanly across multiple lines inside each instrument card so long student names never overflow or get cut off.
  - Dedicated 6-column quick utility toolbar (`History`, `Grant Credit Limit Raise`, `Shop in School Store`, `Print SmartPass`, `Delist/Reinstate`, `Delete`) positioned cleanly below the student's name so buttons never interpose over student names.
- **Instant English Reward Dispenser & Continuous Classroom Ledger Journal:** One-click Eurodollar minting and chronological audit trail with filter pills (`All`, `English Grants`, `Store Redemptions`, `Loans`).

### 💳 Virtual SmartPass Portal, Discretionary Engine & Privilege Store
- **Student SmartPass & Credit Telemetry:** Interactive 3D holographic SmartPass card (`Flip 3D` & `Reveal`), SVG circular FICO gauge (`300–850`), Credit Tier Progression ladder, and Didactic English Task Repayment Calculator (`1 Sentence = E$2`, `1 Word = E$1`, `1 Phrasal Verb = E$3`).
- **Award Daily English Eurodollars:** 12-column split glass console with fast denominations (`+10` to `+250 E$`), activity rubric cards, FICO score impact selector, and live SmartPass settlement receipt with optical barcode.
- **School Store & Privilege Treasury:** 8-column privilege catalog + 4-column sticky Glass POS Checkout Cart with instant loan auto-financing when a student's purchase exceeds their current balance.

## 🔧 Installation & Download

Download the binaries from the **Assets** section of the [Latest Release](https://github.com/silencesight/ATM-/releases):

1. **Standalone Binary:** `ATM 4.0.exe` (run directly without installation)
2. **Windows Installer:** `ATM 4.0 Setup.exe` (installs cleanly to Start Menu / Desktop)
3. **MSI Package:** `ATM 4.0_4.0.0_x64_en-US.msi`

*(Note: If Windows SmartScreen displays a blue "Windows protected your PC" notification, click **More Info** -> **Run Anyway**. This is standard for indie classroom utilities!)*
